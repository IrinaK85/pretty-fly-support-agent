# Pretty Fly AI Support Agent — Hackathon Pitch

## The Problem

Every day, Pretty Fly receives 50+ support tickets. Most are routine:
- "Where's my order?" 
- "Will this fit me?"
- "Can I return this?"

**But every answer requires a human agent.** At scale, this becomes expensive. More importantly—**customers wait 12+ hours for answers** while agents handle repetitive work. It's a bottleneck that kills conversion and loyalty.

**The Real Opportunity:** This repetition is exactly what AI excels at. If we could answer sizing questions instantly, approve returns on the spot, and give order tracking in seconds—we unlock both cost savings AND revenue.

---

## The Solution: Claude-Powered Support with Proactive Marketing Integration

We built a two-phase AI support system that handles 72.4% of tickets automatically while humans focus on complex cases.

### Phase 1: Intelligent Support Routing (£9k investment)
- **Auto-approve returns** within 30 days (any customer)
- **Provide instant sizing guidance** with product details
- **Track orders** in real-time
- **Apply discount codes** instantly
- **Escalate** product quality issues to humans for investigation

**Result:** 72.4% auto-resolution, 5-minute average response (vs 12+ hours manual)

### Phase 2: Marketing Channel Integration (£15k investment)
Embed Claude into Google Ads and Meta checkout flows:
- Customer clicks ad, sees "Ask about sizing?" button
- Claude answers in real-time: *"You're 6'2"—I'd size up. Most customers your height love the fit."*
- Customer completes purchase instead of abandoning cart
- **Result:** 10% conversion uplift across channels
