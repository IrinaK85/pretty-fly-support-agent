How Proactive Support Increases Conversions

The Problem: 23-45% of e-commerce visitors abandon their cart due to sizing anxiety. Answering "Will this fit?" at checkout increases confidence and converts hesitant buyers.

The Solution: Proactive Claude support with instant sizing guidance, fit recommendations, and sizing history for returning customers reduces abandonment and increases AOV for size-uncertain purchases.

Proactive Support Tactics

1. Checkout Support
"Need sizing help?" button on product pages. Claude answers fit questions in real-time before purchase.
2. Post-Purchase Guidance
Day 1: Delivery ETA. Day 7: Care tips. Day 14: "How's it fitting?" with upsell guidance for customers who size up/down.
3. Marketing Integration
"Free sizing consultation" offer in ad copy. Reduces friction for size-conscious buyers, increases ROAS.
4. Returning Customer Memory
Claude remembers: "You usually size down" or "This fits oversized". Increases repeat purchase confidence.
5. Live Chat
AI-powered live chat on checkout. Catches abandonment moments. Handles 76% autonomously, escalates complex questions.
6. Feedback Loop
Claude learns from returns data: Which fit comments lead to happy returns? Updates guidance continuously.
